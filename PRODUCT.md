## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Khanh Nguyen for machine learning or software engineering internships. Technical collaborators and other visitors are secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

This site presents Khanh Nguyen's machine learning, reinforcement learning, software, and game projects in a format that is quick to scan but supports deeper review. Visitors should understand his technical focus, see evidence from the strongest projects, open the resume, and find a direct way to make contact.

## Positioning

The portfolio is centered on a specific body of work: a reinforcement learning controller for the Kessler Game environment, a stock-prediction application, a reduced ZeroDCE computer-vision model, a task-parsing tool, and Shifter, a game published on Steam. It presents practical implementation work alongside the models and algorithms.

## Operating Context

- The site is a static multi-page portfolio intended for GitHub Pages and mobile and desktop browsers.
- Visitors scan the homepage, browse the projects page, read the XFC case study, review the resume, and use the contact page when they want to follow up.
- The source uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with local project images, GIFs, video, charts, and PDF assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages cover the homepage, projects, XFC case study, stock application preview, Shifter preview, resume, and contact information.
- The homepage should stay concise; the projects page should be easy to browse; the XFC page can contain technical detail and visual proof.
- Copy should be grounded, specific, concise, and written in a natural voice. Avoid meta commentary about the website and inflated claims.
- The site must remain usable on mobile, including navigation, buttons, media, and the resume page.
- Real metrics and implementation details may be shown when supported by project evidence. Placeholder or demo screenshots for the stock application should be treated as interface previews, not proof of current model performance.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Khanh Nguyen.
- Keep the voice direct, professional, and personal without sounding like marketing copy.
- Let the work and its evidence carry the claim instead of relying on broad labels or polished filler.
- Preserve room for both technical work and shipped game work without presenting every project as the same kind of engineering effort.

## Evidence on Hand

- XFC visuals and artifacts are in `XFC/`, including gameplay GIFs, a training video, pipeline diagram, dashboards, trajectories, reward curves, and scenario results.
- Shifter visuals and its Steam trailer are in `Shifter/`.
- Stock application screenshots are `screenshot1.png`, `screenshot2.png`, and `screenshot3.png`; they document the interface and include placeholder/demo output.
- The resume is `KhanhNguyen_Resume.pdf`.
- The site links to the Task Parser Assistant repository, the ZeroDCE project repository, the Kessler Game environment, GitHub, LinkedIn, and the Shifter Steam page.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the strongest and most specific work.
- Show implementation evidence wherever it is available.
- Keep the first view fast to understand and make deeper detail optional.
- Describe projects accurately, including limits of placeholder or demo material.
- Keep technical depth and personal voice in balance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Mobile support is a required part of the site experience.
- Images, GIFs, video, links, and interactive controls should remain understandable and usable across screen sizes.
